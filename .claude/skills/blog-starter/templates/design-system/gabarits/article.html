"Giving the game that feeling of constant renewal": Team Empreintes tells us more about The Granny Detective Society

A conversation with Jaximul and Vidu, the founders of Angoulême-based studio Team Empreintes, about their detective game that's as tender as it is ambitious — coming to PC in early 2027.

A By Alex ·JULY 25, 2026 ·6 MIN
Square 300×300
(hero)

Founded in 2024 in Angoulême, Team Empreintes is a young independent studio working on its first title: The Granny Detective Society. You play as Madeleine, a little retired mouse recruited by a sisterhood of granny detectives to solve the mysteries of her neighborhood. An unusual pitch, carried by a soft art direction and a real ambition when it comes to investigation mechanics. We chatted about it with Jaximul and Vidu, the studio's two leads.

Brilliant inspirations for a game with an unusual mood

It's hard to talk about modern detective games without naming a few references. Team Empreintes doesn't hide it: the studio draws on Return of the Obra Dinn, Duck Detective and The Case of the Golden Idol for the structure of its puzzles — that art of cross-referencing scattered clues until the reveal.

But where those titles often cultivate a cold or retro aesthetic, The Granny Detective Society fully embraces a "stylized, soft and tender" look. A deliberate contrast: the rigorous substance of the investigation, the warm form of a fairy tale. It's that meeting point that gives the game its instantly recognizable identity.

"We wanted to give the game that feeling of constant renewal, so that each investigation surprises the player all over again."
Ad space · in-article

A gameplay loop that promises to be gripping

The heart of the game rests on three actions: observing a scene, connecting the information you've gathered, then deducing the truth. Nothing new on paper — but Team Empreintes adds a clever twist: several unlockable "grannytectives", each offering a different perspective on the same scenes. Enough to reread a situation from a fresh angle and, precisely, keep that famous feeling of renewal alive.

To guide the player, an investigation journal centralizes discoveries, while crosswords punctuate the progression. The studio puts a lot of emphasis on accessibility: several paths lead to the right conclusions, with no unfair traps or frustrating dead ends. The goal isn't to trick you, but to guide you toward the aha moment.

Key takeaway

Each unlocked "grannytective" replays the scenes from a new angle: the game aims to be replayable and never static.

Length, release window and closing words

Expect between 6 and 8 hours to see the adventure through — a tight format, in keeping with the ambition of a polished first game rather than a padded one. Release is planned for the first quarter of 2027 on PC; a Switch port is being considered if sales follow.

To wrap up, the developers shared a memorable moment with us: a message from an 82-year-old player, moved by the positive and caring representation of older people in the game. A reaction that neatly sums up the spirit of The Granny Detective Society — a detective game, yes, but above all a game with a lot of heart.

Note

The Granny Detective Society is developed by Team Empreintes (Angoulême). Target release: Q1 2027 on PC, Switch port under consideration.
