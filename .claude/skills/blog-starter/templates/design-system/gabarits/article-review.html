Build overview

The Warden has gone from a purely defensive role to a genuine offensive threat since the patch 3.2 rework. This build leans on crit rate and crit damage to maximize damage spikes, while keeping enough survivability to grind through end-game content.

The class is very forgiving: the rotation window is wide. It's an excellent first DPS to ramp up your power without frying your nerves.

Requirements

Level 70+ with at least one legendary set. For leveling up, see our Warden leveling guide.

Gear & stats

F2P tip

No legendary? The Rampart set (purple, farmable) only loses ~8% damage. Enough for all non-ranked content.

Weaknesses

  • Average mobility

  • Nerf announced for patch 3.5

Talent tree

The tree is built around the central offensive branch. Invest in this order:

  1. Sure Strike — increases base crit rate.
  2. Killing Momentum — crits reduce your ultimate's cooldown.
  3. Second Wind — a safety net against silly deaths.
  4. Apex — the final node that unlocks the real potential.
Rectangle 300×250 · mid-article

Combat rotation

The rotation is deliberately simple — that's the class's strength. On loop:

Mark → Heavy Strike ×2 → Perfect Dodge → Ultimate

The only subtlety: never fire the ultimate without the +15% window opened by a perfect dodge.

F2P alternatives

This entire build is playable without spending a cent: slightly less raw damage, a longer grind, and 100% of the content accessible. See also our complete DPS tier list.

FAQ

Is this build F2P-viable?
Yes, fully. The farmable "Rampart" set only loses ~8% damage compared to the legendary.
What's the minimum level to run it?
Level 70+ with at least one complete legendary or purple gear set.
Does the build hold up after patch 3.5?
A nerf to the ultimate is announced. The page will be updated as soon as the values are confirmed.
